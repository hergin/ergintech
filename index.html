Questions

Common questions

How much does custom software cost?

It depends on the problem, so I don't quote before I understand it. The first meeting is free, and in most cases you see a working model before you commit. We start with one small piece and grow from what works.

How long does it take?

A first prototype can be ready in just a few days, sometimes in a few hours. The full tool depends on its size, but it usually takes a few weeks.

Do I have to share my company's data?

A small, anonymized sample is enough to start. Nothing sensitive has to leave your company for us to find out whether this is worth doing.

Is software built by AI agents reliable?

The agents write the code and the tests. I review every change, and nothing reaches you until it passes. Behind that review are more than 15 years of building software in industry, and teaching and researching software engineering as a professor. I answer for the result.

Who owns the software, and who maintains it?

You own the code and the documentation. Afterwards I can maintain it for you, or teach your team to.

Can the AI run on our own servers?

Yes. For companies that can't send documents to a cloud service, I measure how well AI running on your own hardware does the job before recommending it.

Will it work with the systems we already have?

Yes. Connecting to what you already use is usually part of the job, whether that is an ERP, spreadsheets or files that arrive by email. I start by reading from those systems, so nothing you rely on changes until you are ready.

What if off-the-shelf software would do the job?

Then I will tell you. I check whether something simpler already solves the problem before building anything, and the first meeting costs you nothing either way.
